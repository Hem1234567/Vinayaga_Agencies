Shipment Guidelines

Prepare Your Shipment Correctly.

Good preparation avoids delays and refusals. Final acceptance always rests with the courier carrier.

Documents

  • Full sender and receiver names with complete address and PIN code
  • Working contact numbers for both parties
  • Secure, sealed cover for invoices, letters and agreements
  • Keep a copy of important paperwork

Non-Document Goods

  • Strong, sealed packaging suited to the product
  • Accurate description of contents and quantity
  • Share product details before dispatch
  • Oils and chemicals: carrier approval is mandatory

International Shipments

  • Destination country and full address
  • Invoice and documents required by the carrier and customs
  • Check destination restrictions in advance
  • Duties and charges vary by carrier and country
